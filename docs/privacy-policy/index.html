Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Glint is a wallpaper app for Seeker. It does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information.

App state, wallpaper previews, and selected wallpaper files may be stored locally on your device. Glint does not operate a hosted account service for this site.

App stores, browsers, GitHub Pages, or remote wallpaper catalog and image hosts may process information under their own policies when you use their services.

To request removal of information you believe was provided to Glint, contact the project maintainer through the Glint GitHub repository.
